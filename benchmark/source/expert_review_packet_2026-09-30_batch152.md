# Expert Review Packet: Batch 152

## Scope and status

Eight official ClinicalTrials.gov study packages at the Batch 152 review-queue head were reviewed against prospective-version, outcome-uninformed, protocol-leakage, study-family and document-type gates. Native text was extracted from all 1,010 physical pages in the 16 selected PDFs, and 138 decisive one-based physical pages were rendered and visually inspected in eight contact sheets. All eight candidates were excluded; all decisions remain first-pass screening requiring independent expert review.

## Decisions

- NCT03496623 — exclude: the selected protocol and SAP were produced during conduct to govern COVID-driven missing-data adaptations, including a possible crossover-to-parallel switch and replacement primary endpoint, while the protocol exposes the populations, MMRM, gatekeeping, sample sizes and interim efficacy/futility program.
- NCT04161131 — exclude: the one-page alleged SAP follows study completion and describes the analyses in the past tense, including tests used, effect sizes calculated and focus-group data analyzed. It is an executed methods summary rather than a prospective SAP.
- NCT05398445 — exclude: the SAP follows actual primary completion, and the selected protocol already exposes the primary estimands, rescue/discontinuation strategies, missing-data framework, comparison structure, sample-size and analysis timing.
- NCT03189745 — exclude: the SAP was approved almost eleven months after study completion and depends on observed immunogenicity from the primary and persistence studies; the protocol already exposes endpoint definitions, sample-size precision, analysis cohorts, transformations, missingness and statistical methods.
- NCT03975660 — exclude: the one-page alleged SAP follows study completion by almost a year and mixes future tense with the past-tense statement that the comparisons performed included three selected cutoffs. It is a post-completion executed methods note.
- NCT02848326 — exclude: SAP Amendment 2 follows primary and study completion and uses a common standard deviation estimated from blinded interim data; the protocol already exposes the populations, endpoint construction, graphical multiplicity, sample size and no-interim declaration.
- NCT01829711 — exclude: Final SAP Version 5 follows primary completion, while the protocol already exposes the ITT/evaluable/safety populations, exact confidence-interval primary analysis, sample-size power, time-to-event censoring, safety, PK, immunogenicity and DMC program.
- NCT03558074 — exclude: the protocol and SAP were issued during enrollment, and the protocol records realized conduct such as the central reader not being used and changes the analysis hypothesis and alpha; it independently exposes the datasets, primary and safety analyses, interim safety review and sample size.

## Gate interpretation

- A nominal SAP label does not rescue a one-page post-completion methods summary written in the past tense.
- Blinded conduct information is still execution information when it supplies trial-specific variability estimates or triggers changes to design, endpoint, population or analysis.
- A later extension or booster analysis cannot be treated as an independent prospective target when it incorporates observed primary/persistence-study state.
- A pre-completion target still fails when the model-visible protocol substantially reconstructs the executable program; during-conduct revisions and realized operational facts strengthen that decision.
- Registry actual dates were used only for chronology and never as prospective sample-size targets.
