# Expert Review Packet — Batch 30

Batch 30 produced no promotion. Six SAP packages were finalized after actual primary completion, one package is an execution-informed retrospective protocol/SAP pair, and one same-day protocol/SAP pair has an irreconcilable design and sample-size conflict. All 16 source PDFs were obtained from ClinicalTrials.gov, hashed, page-counted, text-extracted and visually checked at the cover and decision-defining pages.

## Screening decisions

- NCT04038697: hold_version_reconciliation. The same-day documents conflict between a 54-participant protocol design and a 120-participant SAP design.
- NCT03712449: excluded_wrong_document_type. The SAP postdates actual completion and is explicitly oriented to the Clinical Study Report; protocol physical pages 54-56 also expose the target analysis.
- NCT03136484: excluded_wrong_document_type. The internally dated SAP postdates primary completion; protocol physical pages 81-94 also contain the primary estimand and executable analysis.
- NCT02185014: excluded_wrong_document_type. The SAP postdates actual primary and study completion; protocol physical pages 66-69 also expose the target analysis.
- NCT03866577: excluded_wrong_document_type. The post-completion SAP is packaged as CSR Appendix 16.1.9; protocol physical pages 70-72 also expose the target analysis.
- NCT02698475: excluded_wrong_document_type. The SAP postdates actual primary completion; protocol physical pages 48-50 also expose the target analysis.
- NCT02413372: excluded_wrong_document_type. The SAP postdates actual primary and study completion; protocol physical pages 64-67 also expose the target analysis.
- NCT07174635: excluded_wrong_document_type. Both documents postdate completion by more than a year and report completed conduct and analyses in the past tense.
