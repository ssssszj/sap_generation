# Expert Review Packet — Batch 36

## Scope

Batch 36 reviewed eight ClinicalTrials.gov protocol/SAP pairs. No pair passed all prospective-version, identity, independence and protocol-leakage gates. Four pairs were excluded because the public analysis artifacts are retrospective, post-primary, post-completion, execution-informed or part of an already represented rollover family; four were excluded because the model-visible protocol exposes the executable target plan.

All 16 PDFs were hash-frozen and text-extracted. The scan-only 54-page NCT03733314 SAP was fully OCRed. Covers, version histories and decision-defining statistical pages were rendered and visually checked before exclusion.

## Wrong-document-type and independence exclusions

- NCT02036970 — SAP Version 3.0 is post-completion and adds exposure-selected mITT, pooling, subgroup and visit-window rules for regulatory filing.
- NCT02736955 — the SAP is post-completion and incorporates early termination; the study is also a direct rollover from the already represented NBI-98854-1402/NCT02405091 family.
- NCT04681664 — the protocol is only a procedure summary and the two-page SAP describes executed methods, introduces an unsupported reference group and has an internally inconsistent software chronology.
- NCT03733314 — both governing documents postdate primary completion, and SAP Final Version 3.0 changes Bayesian-primary-endpoint initialization after that date.

## Protocol-leakage exclusions

- NCT01084252 — protocol and SAP cover incompatible Phase 1/2 versus Phase-1-only scopes, while the protocol itself exposes populations, sample sizes, alpha, exact-binomial rules and analyses.
- NCT03443024 — the protocol exposes populations, Week-16 analysis, MCMC seeds, NRI/rescue sensitivity and visit rules; the SAP is additionally post-primary.
- NCT02548351 — the protocol exposes populations, CMH/log-rank/Cox methods, missingness, alpha allocation and O'Brien-Fleming monitoring.
- NCT02442765 — the protocol exposes the SPCD weighted statistic, MMRM/covariance details, populations, LOCF/MI sensitivity, dose contrasts, alpha and gatekeeping intent.

## Review gate

No Batch 36 item enters the draft benchmark. The eight exclusions must be replenished from the metadata pool; none is eligible for expert adjudication or a frozen split.
