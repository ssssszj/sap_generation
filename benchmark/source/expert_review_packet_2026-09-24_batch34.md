# Expert Review Packet — Batch 34

## Scope

Batch 34 reviewed eight ClinicalTrials.gov protocol/SAP pairs. None met the benchmark admission gate: six model-visible protocols disclose the target statistical plan, and two public SAPs are post-completion execution-informed versions. No study or requirement count is increased.

All 16 PDFs were hash-frozen and text-extracted. Covers, version pages and the decisive protocol statistical or SAP revision pages were rendered and visually checked before exclusion.

## Protocol-leakage exclusions

- `NCT04087655` — the two-page SAP repeats the protocol's power and statistical-analysis sections; both expose t tests/ANOVAs, repeated-measures factors, post-hoc tests, correlations, subgroup analyses and alpha.
- `NCT04762277` — protocol Final Version 2.0 includes analysis sets, actual-treatment attribution, MMRM, rescue-therapy censoring, MAR/NRI, stratified blocked randomization, interim analyses and detailed sample-size logic.
- `NCT03297541` — the two-page SAP is a verbatim extraction of the protocol's sample-size and analysis sections, including ITT mixed models, AIC selection and least-squares-mean tests.
- `NCT03524092` — the 160-page protocol amendment already contains ITT/PP populations, primary models, NRI/MMRM, graphical multiplicity, power assumptions and DMC/database-lock rules.
- `NCT03392168` — protocol Amendment 2.0 already contains all analysis populations, alpha/power, primary and secondary models, missing-data sensitivity and safety/PK summaries.
- `NCT03802565` — protocol Amendment 1 contains ITT/safety/PP populations, the dose-trend repeated-measures model, ANCOVA/MMRM, LOCF/observed-data sensitivity, safety summaries and detailed power scenarios.

## Post-completion SAP exclusions

- `NCT05148299` — public SAP Final Version 3.0 is dated 2025-02-27 after actual primary/study completion on 2024-12-08; its history adds and revises endpoints, visits, baseline summaries, AE categories and an administrative interim analysis.
- `NCT02765802` — public FINAL v3 SAP was revised 2020-04-01 after primary completion on 2018-07-20 and study completion on 2018-12-12; it adds post-protocol response definitions and changes analysis populations.

## Review gate

No Batch 34 item is suitable for a protocol-only generation benchmark. These records should stay excluded when the candidate pool is refreshed; replacement candidates must pass the same prospective-version and non-leakage checks.
