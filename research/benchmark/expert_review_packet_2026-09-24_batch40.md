# Expert Review Packet — Batch 40

## Scope

Batch 40 reviewed eight ClinicalTrials.gov protocol/SAP families. None passed the independent-target gate. Each model-visible protocol contains an executable statistical plan; four families also have a post-primary SAP, sub-study scope mismatch, or compound version bundle.

All 16 PDFs and eight registry records were downloaded and hash-frozen. Full text was extracted for every protocol and seven SAPs; the NCT03584724 SAP is scan-only, but protocol leakage is independently decisive. Covers and decisive statistical pages were rendered and visually checked.

## Exclusions

- `NCT00862979` — protocol pages 57, 60–61 and 65 expose populations, ANCOVA, chi-square/Kaplan-Meier, missingness and power; the SAP is post-completion.
- `NCT04006457` — SAP scope is only the vaccine sub-study, and protocol pages 169–170 already disclose that sub-study's FAS, descriptive analysis, precision and interim rules.
- `NCT02564926` — protocol pages 44–48 expose FAS/PPS/safety, LOCF, ANCOVA/MMRM/logistic/Fisher tests, confidence intervals and interim rules; the SAP is post-completion.
- `NCT03931785` — protocol pages 78–91 expose ITT, MMRM, alpha/multiplicity, derivations, futility and power; the SAP is post-completion.
- `NCT02895945` — the 430-page protocol bundle repeatedly exposes SAS/FAS/PPAS, no imputation, exact Clopper-Pearson intervals and descriptive analyses; the SAP is post-completion.
- `NCT03584724` — protocol pages 47–50 expose ITT/safety, Poisson GLMM, logistic/ANCOVA, alpha, power and multiplicity.
- `NCT02612909` — protocol pages 67–71 expose FA/PP, Fisher/chi-square, alpha, multiplicity, missingness and complete vaccine power calculations.
- `NCT02081391` — protocol pages 78–84 expose FAS/PPS, ANOVA, Bayesian support, imputation, multiplicity, Kaplan-Meier and power.

## Review gate

No Batch 40 candidate enters the draft benchmark. All eight slots must be replenished; none may be assigned to train/dev/test.
