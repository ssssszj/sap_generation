# Expert Review Packet - Batch 103

## Scope and decision boundary

Batch 103 reviewed seven metadata candidates from the public ClinicalTrials.gov queue. All 14 PDFs and seven registry records were downloaded sequentially, SHA-256 frozen and parsed page by page. Protocol and SAP covers plus the decisive chronology, executed-analysis and leakage pages were rendered from the original PDFs and visually inspected. None passes the benchmark's prospective, outcome-uninformed and independent-hidden-target gates. All seven slots are replenished so the intake remains 96 public first-pass drafts plus 24 metadata candidates.

## Exclusions

- NCT03969563 — both covers claim a pre-recruitment 2019-10-31 date, but the protocol says six class rounds were conducted and participants completed them, while the analysis document says preliminary analyses examined differences, post-hoc analyses were completed and named models were used. These are retrospective executed-method summaries rather than prospective governing documents.
- NCT03136107 — protocol physical pages 15-18 expose the SPF derivation, sequential 10-to-20 valid-result rule, five-invalid-result allowance, plus-or-minus-17% confidence-interval criterion, 13.7-17.7 reference acceptance range and rejection rule. The separate SAP is substantially reconstructable.
- NCT04421014 — protocol physical pages 53-55 expose the same sample size, mITT and 80% PP populations, evaluability criteria, alpha, repeated-measures mixed model, group-by-time term, covariate selection rule, safety summaries and subgroup analyses as the five-page SAP. Exact date and protocol-number matching do not cure complete target leakage.
- NCT02778867 — SAP dated 2019-10-14 follows actual primary completion on 2019-04-17 and final completion on 2019-05-29; no prospective freeze is established.
- NCT02854059 — signed Final SAP 1.0 dated 2017-09-22 follows the terminated study's actual primary and final completion in January 2017.
- NCT02472795 — the selected plan is explicitly a final SAP for CSR dated 2017-04-20 after 2017-01-23 primary and 2017-02-28 final completion, and it records an already-conducted IDMC interim review and recommendation.
- NCT04523831 — the 2020-08-26 final-analysis pair postdates actual primary completion on 2020-08-22; the SAP reports observed enrollment and discontinuations, an already-completed primary analysis and DSM review of results on 2020-08-25.
